About Our Camp

Every summer, Camp Wilderness brings together kids from all over to learn outdoor skills, build lifelong friendships, and create unforgettable memories. Located right in the heart of the majestic pine forests, we offer a safe and thrilling environment.

Camp Activities

We believe in learning by doing. Here are some of the activities our campers enjoy:

  • Archery & Outdoor Survival
  • Kayaking & Lake Swimming
  • Arts, Crafts, & Pottery
  • Nightly Campfires & Storytelling
